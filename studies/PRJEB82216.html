De novo mutation in four stickleback families

Three-spined stickleback · Gasterosteus aculeatus

PRJEB82216

Study overview

Four pairs of unrelated parents each contributed ten full-sib offspring. Whole-genome resequencing of eight parents and forty offspring yielded 115 distinct de novo mutations.
