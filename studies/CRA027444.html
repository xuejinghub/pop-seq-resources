Germline mutation in a two-generation Florida lancelet pedigree

Florida lancelet · Branchiostoma floridae

CRA027444

Study overview

One breeding pair and its 104 F1 offspring were sequenced using high-depth short reads. Allele-aware assemblies of the parents served as references for mutation discovery. Supplementary Table S6 reports calls for individual offspring.
