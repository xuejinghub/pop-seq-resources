GVM001075

Study overview

The study sequenced 643 dogs from 54 multigenerational families, forming 404 parent–offspring trios. After coverage filtering, 390 trios entered the de novo mutation analysis. The run manifest covers the cited NCBI project; its BioSample count is an archive-wide total.
